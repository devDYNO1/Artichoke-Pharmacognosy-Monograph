Pharmacognosy • Herbal Drug Monograph

ARTICHOKE

Cynara cardunculus L. (syn. Cynara scolymus L.)

Family: Asteraceae  •  Arabic: خرشوف

1. Identity 2. Part & Habitat 3. Chemistry 4. Pharmacology 5. Indications 6. Preparations 7. Safety

1 BOTANICAL IDENTITY

Taxonomy
  • Official Drug: Artichoke leaf (Cynarae folium)
  • Common & Arabic: Globe artichoke  |  خرشوف
  • Origin: Mediterranean region, North Africa, Southern Europe, Canary Islands

2 MEDICINAL PART & HABITAT

Morphology
Mediterranean
  • Medicinal Part: Fresh or dried basal leaves (distinct from edible flower receptacle & lower bracts)
  • Habitat: Warm-temperate / subtropical; widely cultivated around the Mediterranean
  • Plant Habit: Perennial herb (up to 1.5 m), deeply divided leaves & purple flower heads

3 CHEMICAL CONSTITUENTS

Phytochemistry
Phenolic Core
  • Phenolic acids: Caffeic acid, chlorogenic acid, cynarin
  • Sesquiterpene lactones: Cynaropicrin, dehydrocynaropicrin, grosheimin
  • Flavonoids: Luteolin glycosides (scolymoside, cynaroside)
  • Others: Essential oil components, phytosterols

4 ACTIONS & PHARMACOLOGY

Activity
Choleretic (↑ Bile)
  • Choleretic: Promotes bile flow from liver & gallbladder
  • Digestive: Relieves fullness, bloating, and flatulence
  • Antioxidant: Linked to phenolic acids & flavonoids
  • Lipid-lowering: Possible effect (clinical evidence varies)

5 INDICATIONS & USE

  • EMA: Traditional relief of dyspepsia / indigestion, fullness, bloating & flatulence
  • EDA: Also lists hyperlipidemia and choleretic use
Clinical Note: Traditional use does not prove clinical efficacy; not a proven treatment for heart or liver disease.

6 PREPARATIONS

  • Forms: Dried-leaf infusion (tea), powdered leaf, dry/soft/liquid extracts
  • Dosing: Preparations are NOT interchangeable; follow product directions
Drug Interactions: Coumarin anticoagulants • Iron absorption • ↓ Blood glucose (with antidiabetics)

7 SAFETY & CAUTIONS

  • Side effects: Diarrhea, cramps, nausea, heartburn
  • Avoid in: Asteraceae allergy or bile-duct obstruction
  • Seek advice: Gallstones & liver/biliary disorders
Pregnancy & Lactation Not recommended (insufficient data)
EDA Age Limit Not recommended < 12 years
